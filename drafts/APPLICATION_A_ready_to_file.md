```
✓ COPY AS-IS — paste this entire block into the RTI text box

Under the Right to Information Act, 2005, I request the following information from the Office of the Executive Engineer, BBMP Mahadevapura Division, regarding road infrastructure works executed in Ward No. 150 (Bellandur) and its constituent areas (Green Glen Layout, Kaikondrahalli, Kasavanahalli, Haralur, Junnasandra) for the financial years 2018-19 to 2024-25.

1. DATABASE OF EXECUTED WORKS:
Provide a certified tabular list (Excel/CSV preferred) of ALL road works (Asphalting, Concreting, Pothole filling, Drain remodeling, Footpath works) executed in the ward. For EACH work, please provide:
a) Job Code / P-Code (e.g., 150-19-0000XX).
b) Name of Work (Administrative Sanction Description).
c) Name of the Agency/Contractor (Legal Entity Name).
d) Work Order (WO) Number and Date.
e) Work Order Amount (Sanctioned Cost).
f) Actual Completion Date (as per Measurement Book).
g) Defect Liability Period (DLP) Status: Start Date and End Date of DLP for each work.

2. EXPENDITURE AND ACCOUNTABILITY:
For each work in Item 1:
a) Total Amount Paid to the contractor to date (Gross Bill Amount).
b) Name and Designation of the officials (AE, AEE, Executive Engineer) who were the "Check Measurement" officers for each work.
c) A copy of the "DLP Register" or "Road History Register" for Ward 150 showing the current liability status of all roads.

3. CONTACT INFORMATION:
The current official contact details (Name, Designation, Office Address, Official Mobile, Official Email) for:
a) The Executive Engineer, Mahadevapura Division.
b) The Assistant Executive Engineer (AEE) for the Bellandur Sub-division.
c) The Assistant Revenue Officer (ARO) for Bellandur.

4. FORMAT:
I request data in digital format (Excel/CSV via email or CD) under Section 4(1)(b) of the RTI Act. If "Road History" data is available on the BBMP Road History 2.0 portal, please provide the direct URL and the list of available data attributes for Ward 150.

Note: If any road (such as ORR or Sarjapur Main Road) falls under "Projects Central" or "Major Roads" jurisdiction, please provide available Ward Road data from your office and transfer only the relevant part of this request under Section 6(3). Kindly do not transfer the entire application.
```
