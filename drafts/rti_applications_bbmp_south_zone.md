# RTI Applications for Road Infrastructure Transparency - BBMP South Zone

**Target Portal:** Karnataka RTI Online (rtionline.karnataka.gov.in)
**Public Authority:** Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike (South Zone) BBMP
**Fee:** Rs. 10 per application (exempted for BPL card holders)

---

## APPLICATION 1: Road Inventory & Construction History

**Public Authority:** Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike (South Zone) BBMP

### Text for RTI Request (2,847 characters)

```
Subject: Information regarding complete road inventory and construction history in BBMP South Zone

Under the Right to Information Act, 2005, I request the following information pertaining to all roads (arterial, sub-arterial, and ward roads) under the jurisdiction of BBMP South Zone:

1. Please provide a complete list of all roads in BBMP South Zone including:
   a) Road name/identification number
   b) Road length (in kilometers)
   c) Road width
   d) Road type (arterial/sub-arterial/ward road)
   e) Ward number and ward name under which each road falls
   f) Current road surface type (asphalt/concrete/WBM/unpaved)

2. For each road, please provide construction history including:
   a) Year of original construction
   b) Name and registered address of the contractor who constructed the road
   c) Contract/Work order number and date
   d) Total sanctioned cost for construction
   e) Actual expenditure incurred
   f) Date of completion of work
   g) Name and designation of the Executive Engineer who supervised the work

3. For roads constructed or reconstructed in the last 10 years (2016-2026), please also provide:
   a) Tender notification number and date
   b) Number of bids received
   c) Name of the contractor awarded the work
   d) Bid amount quoted by the winning contractor
   e) Defect Liability Period specified in the contract

4. Please provide the current designated officer (name, designation, contact number, and email) responsible for road infrastructure in each subdivision of South Zone (Jayanagar, Basavanagudi, BTM Layout, Padmanabhanagar, Vijayanagar, Chickpet).

I prefer to receive this information in electronic format (Excel/CSV spreadsheet) via email if available. If physical copies are required, I am willing to pay the prescribed charges.

This information is sought for public interest purposes to promote transparency in road infrastructure governance.
```

## APPLICATION 2: Road Maintenance & Repair Records

**Public Authority:** Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike (South Zone) BBMP

### Text for RTI Request (2,912 characters)

```
Subject: Information regarding road maintenance, repair works, and current maintenance contracts in BBMP South Zone

Under the Right to Information Act, 2005, I request the following information pertaining to road maintenance in BBMP South Zone:

1. Please provide details of all road maintenance/repair works carried out in South Zone during the last 5 financial years (FY 2021-22 to FY 2025-26) including:
   a) Road name and ward number
   b) Nature of maintenance work (pothole filling/resurfacing/overlay/complete reconstruction)
   c) Name and address of the contractor who executed the work
   d) Work order number and date
   e) Sanctioned estimate amount
   f) Actual amount paid to contractor
   g) Date of completion of work

2. For each maintenance work above, please provide:
   a) Name and designation of the Junior Engineer and Assistant Engineer who supervised the work
   b) Whether measurement book entries were recorded (Yes/No)
   c) Whether quality inspection was conducted before payment (Yes/No)

3. Please provide details of current annual maintenance contracts (AMC) for roads in South Zone:
   a) Contract number and validity period
   b) Name and address of the AMC contractor
   c) Total contract value
   d) Scope of roads covered under each AMC
   e) Performance guarantee amount deposited by contractor

4. For roads still under Defect Liability Period (DLP):
   a) Road name and ward number
   b) Name of original contractor
   c) DLP start date and end date
   d) Security deposit/performance guarantee amount held
   e) Any defects reported and rectification status

5. Please provide the name, designation, office address, phone number, and email ID of:
   a) All Executive Engineers (Roads) in South Zone
   b) All Assistant Executive Engineers (Roads) in South Zone
   c) The nodal officer for road maintenance complaints in South Zone

I prefer to receive this information in electronic format (Excel/CSV) via email.
```

## APPLICATION 3: Budget Allocation & Financial Expenditure

**Public Authority:** Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike (South Zone) BBMP

### Text for RTI Request (2,789 characters)

```
Subject: Information regarding budget allocation and expenditure on road infrastructure in BBMP South Zone

Under the Right to Information Act, 2005, I request the following information pertaining to road infrastructure finances in BBMP South Zone:

1. Please provide ward-wise budget allocation and expenditure for road works for the last 5 financial years (FY 2021-22 to FY 2025-26):
   a) Ward number and ward name
   b) Budget allocated for road construction
   c) Budget allocated for road maintenance
   d) Actual expenditure on road construction
   e) Actual expenditure on road maintenance
   f) Unspent balance, if any

2. Please provide details of all road works sanctioned under the following funding sources in South Zone (for the last 5 years):
   a) Ward Development Grants
   b) MLA/MP Local Area Development funds
   c) State Finance Commission grants
   d) Central Finance Commission grants
   e) BBMP's own funds
   For each, provide: Work description, sanctioned amount, contractor name, and current status.

3. Please provide details of all road works with cost overruns in the last 5 years:
   a) Road name and work order number
   b) Original sanctioned amount
   c) Revised sanctioned amount
   d) Final expenditure
   e) Reason for cost overrun (if recorded)
   f) Authority who approved the cost overrun

4. Please provide details of any road works where contractor payments were delayed beyond 60 days:
   a) Work order number and road name
   b) Contractor name
   c) Bill amount
   d) Date of bill submission
   e) Date of actual payment
   f) Reason for delay (if recorded)

5. Please provide the name, designation, and contact details of:
   a) Chief Accounts Officer responsible for South Zone
   b) Accounts Officer handling road infrastructure payments

I prefer to receive this information in electronic format (Excel/CSV) via email.
```

## APPLICATION 4: Contractor Registration & Performance Records

**Public Authority:** Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike (South Zone) BBMP

### Text for RTI Request (2,856 characters)

```
Subject: Information regarding registered contractors and their performance records for road works in BBMP South Zone

Under the Right to Information Act, 2005, I request the following information:

1. Please provide the list of all contractors registered with BBMP for civil/road works who have executed projects in South Zone during the last 5 years (2021-2026):
   a) Contractor name (individual/firm/company)
   b) Registration number with BBMP
   c) Class/Category of registration
   d) Registered office address
   e) Contact phone number and email (if available in records)
   f) Total number of road works awarded in South Zone
   g) Total value of works awarded in South Zone

2. For each contractor above, please provide performance assessment details:
   a) Number of works completed within stipulated time
   b) Number of works delayed beyond stipulated time
   c) Number of works where quality issues were reported
   d) Number of works where defect rectification was required during DLP
   e) Any blacklisting/suspension/debarment action taken (with dates and reasons)

3. Please provide details of contractors who have been:
   a) Blacklisted in the last 5 years (name, registration number, date, reason)
   b) Issued show-cause notices for poor performance (name, registration number, date, reason)
   c) Had their performance guarantee forfeited (name, registration number, amount, reason)

4. Please provide the criteria/parameters used by BBMP for:
   a) Evaluating contractor performance
   b) Awarding repeat contracts to the same contractor
   c) Blacklisting contractors

5. Please provide details of the Committee/Authority responsible for:
   a) Contractor registration and renewal
   b) Contractor performance evaluation
   c) Blacklisting decisions
   Including names, designations, and contact details of current members.

I prefer to receive this information in electronic format (Excel/CSV) via email.
```

## APPLICATION 5: Road Quality & Citizen Grievances

**Public Authority:** Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike (South Zone) BBMP

### Text for RTI Request (2,698 characters)

```
Subject: Information regarding road quality inspections and citizen grievances in BBMP South Zone

Under the Right to Information Act, 2005, I request the following information:

1. Please provide details of road quality inspections conducted in South Zone during the last 3 years:
   a) Road name and ward number
   b) Date of inspection
   c) Name and designation of inspecting officer
   d) Quality parameters checked
   e) Inspection findings/report summary
   f) Action taken based on inspection (if any)

2. Please provide details of citizen complaints/grievances regarding roads in South Zone received through all channels (Sahaya, BBMP app, written complaints, email) for the last 3 years:
   a) Ward-wise total number of complaints received each year
   b) Category-wise breakdown (potholes, waterlogging, uneven surface, incomplete work, etc.)
   c) Number of complaints resolved within 7 days
   d) Number of complaints resolved within 30 days
   e) Number of complaints pending beyond 30 days
   f) Number of complaints where contractor was held responsible

3. For road works where quality issues were identified post-completion:
   a) Road name and work order number
   b) Nature of quality defect
   c) Action taken against contractor
   d) Rectification status
   e) Financial recovery made, if any

4. Please provide details of any third-party quality audits conducted on road works:
   a) Name of audit agency
   b) Roads/works audited
   c) Date of audit
   d) Key findings (summary)
   e) Action taken on audit observations

5. Please provide name, designation, phone number, and email ID of:
   a) Grievance Redressal Officer for South Zone
   b) Quality Control Officer/Engineer for road works
   c) Citizen facilitation center in-charge for South Zone

I prefer to receive this information in electronic format (Excel/CSV) via email.
```

## NOTES FOR FILING

### Recommended Filing Order
1. Start with Application 1 (Road Inventory) - this establishes the baseline data
2. File Applications 2-5 after receiving response to Application 1, or file simultaneously if time is critical

### Public Authority Selection
For all applications, select: **Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike (South Zone) BBMP**

### Expected Timeline
- Response due within 30 days of filing
- If no response, file First Appeal to First Appellate Authority
- If still unsatisfied, file Second Appeal to Karnataka Information Commission

### Tips for Better Response
1. If response is incomplete, file a fresh RTI citing the original RTI number and asking specifically for missing information
2. Keep copies of all registration numbers and payment receipts
3. If directed to another department, file separately with that department

### Estimated Total Cost
- 5 applications x Rs. 10 = Rs. 50 (application fees)
- Additional charges may apply for copies (Rs. 2 per page)

### Data Usage for Web Portal
The data obtained from these RTIs can be used to create:
- Searchable road database by ward/locality
- Contractor performance dashboard
- Budget utilization tracker
- Grievance resolution metrics
- Officer accountability directory
